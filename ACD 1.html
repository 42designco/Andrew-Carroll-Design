About Andrew

Design, faith,
and a guitar.

Andrew Carroll built his career at the intersection of creativity and mission. With a Bachelor of Fine Arts in Communication Design from Kutztown University, he developed a rare combination of artistic precision, technical depth, and strategic insight — the kind most creatives develop only one of.

His path ran through six years of community ministry leadership at a Life Hope Center in Allentown, Pennsylvania — where he discovered that the most powerful design work isn't the kind that wins awards. It's the kind that helps people show up, be seen, and do what they were made to do.

Today Andrew operates at the intersection he's always inhabited: where beauty meets function, faith meets strategy, and a mission-driven leader finally gets the professional presence their work deserves.
